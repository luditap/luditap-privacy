Privacy Policy for LudiTap

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This policy explains what data is collected when you play LudiTap (“the App” or “the Game”), how it is used, and what rights you have. LudiTap is a free game supported by advertising. You don’t need to create an account to play, and your progress is saved on your own device.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Lucarely, the developer and publisher of LudiTap. For any privacy-related questions, you can write to luditap.lucarely@gmail.com.

2. What data is collected and how

LudiTap does not ask you for personal data directly and has no sign-up, login, or in-app purchases. Your game progress is stored locally on your device.

Some data is collected automatically through third-party services integrated into the App:

  • Advertising (Google AdMob): device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, device type and operating system, and data about how you interact with ads.
  • Analytics (GameAnalytics): gameplay events (such as game session starts, progress, or ads watched), technical device information, a randomly generated identifier, and your approximate location (country).

3. How the data is used and legal basis

  • To show ads (including rewarded ads) that fund the free game.
  • To measure and improve the game’s performance, balance, and playability.
  • To prevent fraud and ensure the App runs securely.

The legal bases (EU General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR) are your consent — for personalized advertising and the use of certain identifiers in the European Union — and legitimate interest in analyzing aggregate use of the game, ensuring its security, and showing non-personalized ads.

4. Advertising

LudiTap shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on your consent.

If you are in the European Union, the European Economic Area, or the United Kingdom, the first time you open the App we show you a screen to manage your consent (through Google’s User Messaging Platform). You can change your choice at any time from the game’s settings or by resetting your consent.

You can also limit personalized advertising from your device settings (by resetting or deleting your advertising ID) and from Google Ads Settings.

5. Who the data is shared with

The data described above is processed through the following providers, which act as processors on behalf of the controller. You can review how they handle data in their own policies:

  • Google (AdMob): Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from its partners.
  • GameAnalytics: GameAnalytics EU Privacy Notice.

Your personal data is not sold to third parties.

6. International transfers

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area (for example, in the United States). Where this happens, such transfers are protected by the safeguards required under the GDPR, such as the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

7. Data retention

Data is kept only for as long as necessary for the purposes described; each provider also applies its own retention periods. Progress saved locally remains on your device until you clear the App’s data or uninstall it.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, object to its processing, request its restriction or portability, and withdraw your consent at any time. To exercise these rights, write to luditap.lucarely@gmail.com.

If you believe your data has not been handled properly, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD, www.aepd.es) or with the supervisory authority in your country.

9. Children’s privacy

LudiTap is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under that age. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us at luditap.lucarely@gmail.com and we will delete it.

10. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data. Please note that no transmission or storage system is completely secure.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date shown above reflects the current version. Significant changes will be communicated through the App or the store listing.

Contact
Questions about this policy or your data? Write to luditap.lucarely@gmail.com.
